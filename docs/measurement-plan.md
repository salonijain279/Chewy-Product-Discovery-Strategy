# Measurement plan

## Primary hypothesis

A transparent, pet-specific shortlist reduces decision effort without decreasing customer control or trust.

## Funnel measures

- Search-to-product-view rate
- Product-view-to-cart rate
- Time to first relevant product
- Search reformulations per session
- External-site exits during discovery
- Recommendation explanation opens

## Quality and trust measures

- Shortlist relevance rating
- User-reported confidence
- Override and filter-adjustment rate
- Unsupported or unsafe recommendation rate
- Accessibility task completion

## Experiment design

Start with a limited A/B test against the standard search experience. Stratify or pre-register subgroup analysis for first-time owners and customers using health-related filters. Monitor conversion alongside trust, override behavior, and safety review outcomes.

## Decision rule

Do not launch based on conversion lift alone. Require stable relevance, no meaningful safety regression, transparent user controls, and acceptable performance across key customer groups.
