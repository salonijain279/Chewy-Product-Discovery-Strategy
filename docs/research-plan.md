# Research plan

## Objective

Understand where pet-commerce customers experience friction before introducing a solution concept.

## Questions

- What information do customers use to judge product fit?
- When does catalog breadth become choice overload?
- Why do shoppers leave the platform to seek advice elsewhere?
- What evidence would make a personalized recommendation trustworthy?
- How do first-time and experienced owners differ?

## Sampling

Use purposive sampling across current users, non-users, experienced owners, first-time owners, and people managing more complex pet needs. The academic project used 15 needs-finding conversations.

## Interview structure

1. Recent shopping story
2. Search and comparison behavior
3. Trust signals and external sources
4. Abandonment or switching moments
5. Desired control and explanation

## Synthesis

- Tag observations separately from interpretations.
- Cluster evidence into repeated behaviors and unmet needs.
- Record contradictory evidence.
- Do not turn one quote into a market-size claim.
- Trace each concept back to a documented need.
