# Responsible product guardrails

- Treat profile attributes as user-controlled inputs, not immutable truths.
- Explain why each item appears and which filters influenced it.
- Separate product discovery from veterinary or clinical advice.
- Provide easy editing, reset, and opt-out controls.
- Minimize retained profile data and define deletion rules.
- Evaluate recommendation quality across pet types and customer segments.
- Route high-risk health questions to qualified sources.
- Log model and rule versions for investigation and rollback.
